# Ship wake

**Commit:** `b66b1ef`. Off by default.

## What changed

A ship under way leaves a wake: the V of waves, the churned water and a trail of foam, as a swimmer's wake at a
larger size and over a longer time.

## How it works

- **Finding a ship.** The game draws a ship's hull as a building with a world matrix of its own, its origin at
  sea level. The DLL follows the origin of every large building draw from frame to frame. One that moves faster
  than 1.5 yards a second at the water is a ship.
- **No new shader constants.** Every constant of the water shader is in use. A ship takes one of the 4 wake slots
  and gives its size in a value that a swimmer's trail does not use.
- **The object list cannot place a ship.** A ship's game object (a transport, type 15) holds 0 as its place.

## Controls

| Control | Tab | Range | Default |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ship Wake | Water | 0 to 20 (0 is off) | 0 |
| Ship Wake Forward | Water | 0 to 60 yards | 15 |
| Ship Wake Depth | Water | 0 to 600% | 200 |

- **Ship Wake:** the wake's size as a multiple of a swimmer's. 10 gives a wake about 40 yards long.
- **Ship Wake Forward:** how far forward along the ship the wake starts. The hull's origin is not its bow.
- **Ship Wake Depth:** how deep the wake's waves are, against a swimmer's.

`comfyfog.ini`: `[water] shipWake`, `shipWakeForward`, `shipWakeDepth`.
