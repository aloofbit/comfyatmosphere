# Grass in the wind

## What changed

comfyfog.dll draws the grass in the wind. It was the separate mod comfygrass until now. The grass sways from its
root, leans away from your character, and rocks, pebbles, shells and bones stay still, as before.

## Install

- Remove the line `comfygrass.dll` from `dlls.txt`, delete `comfygrass.dll` and `dlls.txt.cache`. While
  comfygrass.dll is loaded, its grass is drawn and the Grass controls do nothing.
- The ComfyGrass addon can go: Foliage Density is on the Grass tab.

## Controls

A Grass tab in `/atmos options`, and the same controls on Video > Atmosphere:

| Control | Setting in `comfyfog.ini` |
| --- | --- |
| Grass | `[grass] enabled` |
| Grass Wind (%) | `[grass] scale` |
| Grass Wave Speed (tenths of a yard a second) | `[grass] speed` |
| Grass Wave Length (yards) | `[grass] wavelength` |
| Grass Wind Direction (degrees) | `[grass] directionDeg` |
| Grass Lean (%) | `[grass] lean` |
| Grass Parting (%) | `[grass] forceCenter` |
| Grass Parting Radius (tenths of a yard) | `[grass] radius` |
| Foliage Density | the game's `frillDensity` |

The other comfygrass settings are in the `[grass]` section of `comfyfog.ini`. F11 reloads them. The F9 and F10 keys
are gone: the probe (F12) logs the grass, and the Grass box turns it on and off.

Debug View 30 shows the bend: black where the grass is still, white at the tips.

## Cost

0.12 ms of CPU a frame in Redridge's grass (213 draws, 130,516 vertices), the draws included.
