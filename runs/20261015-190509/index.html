[bench]

The benchmark. Alt+F12 runs four steps: rays + volumetric light + fog, rays + the fog alone, rays, nothing. Each step runs for settle seconds, then is measured for measure seconds. Then the settings go back as they were, and a table goes to comfyatmos.log: frame rate, the slowest 1% of frames, and the GPU and CPU time of our own passes, with the light split into its parts. Play with the light on for a minute first, so its cache is full. Stand still, do not move the mouse, and face the sun. F11 stops it.
